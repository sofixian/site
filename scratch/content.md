**Sofi Xian** (冼納然, aka Sofia Lee)
is a Rotterdam-based semiotician, experimental photographer, and sound artist.

## Semiotics

Sofi is a founder of the [Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute](https://cari.institute) where she first shared her research on Frutiger Aero.  She examines the distribution of symbols and meaning across all media, with a particular focus on cinema, music, and UX design.  Her work is informed by her background in Computational Linguistics, for which she holds a Master's Degree from the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

## Experimental photography

Sofi is the founder of digicam.love.

## Sound art

Sofi is half of the band nyi.na along with Avita Maheen.
