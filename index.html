Sofi Xian (冼納然, also Sofia Lee)
is a Rotterdam-based researcher, cultural theorist, and multimedia artist. Informed by a diverse background including computational linguistics, experimental photography, cinema, and sound art, she charts the semiotic terrain of consumer culture and theorises its evolution. Her particular focuses are on how unpopular, naïve, marginal, repetitive and forgotten detritus of culture serve as potent reservoirs of emergent aesthetics and meaning formations.

As a founding member of the Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute, she has pioneered approaches to 'consumer aesthetics' and contributed foundational research on the aesthetic Frutiger Aero, which she also coined along with Froyo Tam. She has led workshops on consumer aesthetics research practices. She is also a founder of digicam.love, a community founded in 2018 dedicated to highlighting the untapped aesthetic potential of forgotten point-and-shoot digital cameras.
